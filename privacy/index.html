Privacy Policy for Savense

Effective date: 16 November 2026

Publisher and privacy contact

Savense is published by Easeward Studio. For privacy questions, contact savense.support@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

Savense is designed primarily as an on-device personal finance app. Transactions, categories, budgets, recurring entries, accounts/payment sources, smart rules, trips/wallets, preferences and related finance information are stored locally on your device unless you explicitly export or back them up.

Savense does not require you to create a Savense account to use the core app.

Advertising in the Free version

The Free version of Savense may display advertising provided by Google AdMob. To provide and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device information, IP address, advertising identifiers where permitted, app interactions related to advertising, consent choices and other technical information.

Savense uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and store advertising/privacy choices where required, including for users in the EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland. Ads are requested only when the applicable consent status allows them.

Savense Premium does not request or display ads.

For more information about how Google processes data in advertising products, see Google's privacy and advertising documentation.

Advertising privacy choices

Where required, Savense provides access to advertising privacy choices from the app's privacy settings. You may be able to update consent or opt-out choices there, depending on your region and applicable law.

App Tracking Transparency and IDFA

The current version of Savense does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission solely for the purpose of enabling personalized advertising. If Savense adds IDFA-based tracking or an ATT permission request in a future release, this policy and the in-app disclosure will be updated before that feature is enabled.

Location and automatic Travel Wallet

If you enable location-based Travel Wallet features, Savense may request location permission to determine the city or country you are currently in and automatically associate eligible transactions with a matching travel wallet. Location is used for this feature and is not sold by Easeward Studio.

You can deny or revoke location access at any time in iOS Settings. Location-based automation will not work without the required permission.

Apple Wallet, bank notifications and Shortcuts automations

Savense can support user-configured iOS Shortcuts automations that pass notification information into Savense. Depending on the automation you create, this information may include a notification title, subtitle, body, merchant name, payment source and amount.

This information is used to create or prepare transactions in Savense and is stored with your local finance data. Easeward Studio does not operate a server that receives your bank or Apple Wallet notification contents as part of this feature.

Smart Rules and merchant processing

Savense may process merchant names and transaction details locally to apply categories, accounts/payment sources, trips/wallets or other rules you configure. These rules are intended to reduce manual entry and remain part of your local app data.

Subscriptions and purchases

Savense Premium subscriptions and promotional offer codes are processed through Apple's App Store and StoreKit. Easeward Studio receives entitlement and transaction-status information needed to determine whether Premium features should be unlocked, but does not receive your full payment-card details. Apple's own privacy policy applies to App Store purchases.

Exchange-rate service

For automatic currency conversion, Savense may request current or historical reference exchange rates from a third-party exchange-rate service over HTTPS. Such requests may include source currency, target currency and, where relevant, a date. The service provider may receive basic technical information such as your IP address. Savense does not need to include your transaction note, category or budget in an exchange-rate request.

Exports and backups

Backup, CSV and Excel files are created when you request them or when you configure an automation that creates a backup. Files are saved to the location you choose, which may include iCloud Drive or another storage provider available through iOS. Files saved to third-party or cloud storage are subject to that provider's privacy terms.

Easeward Studio does not receive the contents of your exported or backup files merely because you create them.

Biometrics and device security

If you enable App Lock, Savense may use Face ID, Touch ID or the device passcode through Apple's system frameworks. Biometric templates are handled by the operating system and are not made available to Savense.

Support communications

If you contact Easeward Studio by email, we receive the email address, message and any information or attachments you choose to send. Support communications may be retained as reasonably necessary to respond to your request and maintain support records.

Data retention and deletion

Your finance records remain on your device until you delete them, reset them in Savense or remove the app, subject to iOS backup/restore behavior and any copies you separately exported or backed up.

Deleting data from Savense does not automatically delete copies that you previously exported to Files, iCloud Drive or another storage provider.

Children

Savense is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly operate a Savense account service that collects children's finance information.

Your privacy rights

Because core finance records are generally stored locally, you can access, edit and delete most of that information directly in Savense. Where Easeward Studio processes personal data outside your device, such as support communications, applicable privacy law may provide rights including access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection.

Third-party services

Savense may interact with services provided by Apple, Google and exchange-rate providers for features described above. Those companies process data under their own privacy policies and terms.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Savense's features, advertising setup or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
